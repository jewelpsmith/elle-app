Trust + Safety

Elle is an AI guide. It can help with ideas, planning, writing, decisions and everyday questions, but it can make mistakes.

Privacy: Do not share passwords, one-time authentication codes, banking credentials, government identification numbers, or an exact home address with Elle.
Live Elle: A Live Elle request is not a confirmed appointment. The appointment is confirmed only after you choose an available time and Google Calendar confirms it.
PenPal: PenPal is intended for friendship and genuine human connection. It is not a dating, hookup, sexual-content, or adult-services platform.
Safety tools: Members may Block, Report and End Connection.
